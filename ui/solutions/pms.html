What's included

Everything the front desk actually does, every day

Bookings that can't double-book

Create a reservation and the system checks the room's real availability for those dates before it lets you save — no more two guests turning up for the same room on the same night.

Rooms & rate management

Set up room types and rates once, then track every individual room's status — available, occupied, cleaning, maintenance — so front desk always knows what's sellable right now.

A real guest directory

Every guest's details, ID and stay history live in one searchable place — by name, phone or ID number — instead of scattered across old registration slips.

Check-in & check-out, one tap

Checking a guest in marks the room occupied automatically; checking out marks it for cleaning. The room list always reflects reality, not what someone remembered to update.

Multi-branch, one login for owners

Running more than one property? Owners see and switch between every branch; front desk logins are scoped to the branch they actually work at.

Rates that don't shift under you

A booking locks in its rate the moment it's made. If you change a room type's price next month, every booking made before that keeps the price the guest actually agreed to.
