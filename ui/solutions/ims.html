Who it's for

  • Retail shops, hardware stores and stockists with real variety in stock

  • Owners who still count stock by hand at month end

  • Businesses that buy on credit from suppliers and sell on credit to regulars

What's included

From the shelf to the ledger, all of it

Stock down to the variant

A product isn't just one number on the shelf — track size, colour or model as its own line, each with its own stock count, cost and selling price.

Purchase orders that update stock

Record a purchase from a supplier and the stock count updates the same moment — no separate "now go update the register" step.

Supplier & customer ledgers

Every supplier you buy on credit from and every customer you sell on credit to has a running balance, built from their actual purchases, sales and payments — not a notebook you have to keep finding.

Fiscal-year-correct VAT invoices

Invoices are numbered and dated against the right Bikram Sambat fiscal year automatically. VAT applies if you're registered for it — at your real rate, worked out for you, not by hand.

Quotations that turn into sales

Send a price quote before a customer commits, then convert it straight into an invoice once they say yes — no re-typing the same list twice.

Multi-branch stock, one view

Running more than one branch? See stock levels across all of them, or drill into one — owners aren't limited to a single location's view.

Low-stock alerts

Set a reorder point per variant and see what needs restocking before you actually run out.

Margin & stock reports

See what's actually making you money, not just what's moving — cost price and selling price are tracked side by side.

A counter-ready sale screen

Search or scan, add to the sale, take payment — built for the pace of an actual counter, not a back-office form.
