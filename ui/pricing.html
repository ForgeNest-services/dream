How it actually works

Billing, the honest version

1

Try free for 30 days

Each app's trial starts the moment you create your first staff login for it — not the day you sign up. Try one app today, another next month.

2

Pay however you already do

Nepal doesn't have reliable auto-recurring card billing yet, so we don't pretend to. Pay by the methods you already use, and send us confirmation — no card on file, ever.

3

We confirm, you're live

Our team confirms your payment and activates the app, usually the same day. If your trial runs out before you've paid, the app locks until it's sorted — nothing is ever deleted.
