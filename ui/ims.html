What's inside

Stock control that doesn't need a spreadsheet

From the SKU catalog to the purchase bill that restocked it, every movement is logged the moment it happens.

SKU catalog with variants

Every product with its own unit, reorder threshold, and variant set, a shirt in three sizes is one product, not three entries to keep straight.

Purchase orders & suppliers

Raise a purchase bill against a supplier and stock updates the moment it's received, no separate step to remember.

Low-stock alerts

Every item has its own reorder point, checked per branch, so you find out you're low before a customer does.

Stock movement history

Restock, adjustment, or transfer, every change is logged with who made it, so a stock count discrepancy is a lookup, not a mystery.

VAT-aware sales & invoicing

Sales bills compute VAT automatically once you're VAT-registered, with dates in Bikram Sambat and a fiscal-year invoice sequence that never skips a number.

Multi-branch visibility
