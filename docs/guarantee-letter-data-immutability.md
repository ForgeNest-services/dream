# Guarantee Letter

**Subject:** Guarantee of Data Immutability in Srota IMS

**Software Name:** Srota IMS  
**Developed by:** Forgenest Services Pvt. Ltd.  
**Date:** [DATE]

---

To,  
The Inland Revenue Department of Nepal  
Kathmandu, Nepal

---

We, **Forgenest Services Pvt. Ltd.**, hereby guarantee that **Srota IMS** (Inventory Management System) does not permit amendment, editing, or deletion of any transaction data once an invoice has been issued.

In compliance with **विद्युतीय बीजक सम्बन्धी कार्यविधि, २०८२**, clause 6.3(घ), the software enforces the following:

1. Finalized invoices cannot be edited or deleted from the front-end or the back-end.
2. Corrections are made exclusively through Credit Notes, which are recorded separately with their own sequential number.
3. Immutability is enforced at the database level and cannot be bypassed, including by administrative connections.
4. All transactions are recorded in an append-only audit log.

We take full responsibility for the accuracy of this statement.
